# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Recruiters, hiring managers, prospective clients and fellow developers in Malaysia who want to judge, in a minute or two, whether Dol can build and run real production web platforms. They arrive from a shared link (WhatsApp, LinkedIn, résumé) mostly on phones, sometimes on a desktop at work.

## Product Purpose
Personal portfolio of Dol, a full-stack Blazor Server / .NET developer. It shows the platforms he has built (MIRAR, PNMS, iSelia, iSeliaIntel, CareVentis, star8892, Autumn Twilight, iSusun), how he works, and gives a way to contact him or download his résumé. Success: a visitor opens a case study and then emails or messages him.

## Positioning
He builds whole systems end to end — public site redesign plus the entire admin back office (certificates, quizzes, surveys, enquiries, audit logs, scrapers) — on one consistent stack, and keeps maintaining them in production.

## Operating Context
Static HTML/CSS/JS, no build step, deployed to GitHub Pages / Vercel. Shared nav/footer inlined in every page. Command palette (/ or Ctrl+K), theme toggle, Formspree contact form, giscus guestbook, 404 snake game, Konami/"sus" easter eggs.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Must stay a plain static site with no build step; any library must load from a CDN or be vendored.
- Light and dark themes, following the OS until the visitor picks.
- All existing pages, URLs, copy facts and features stay.

## Brand Commitments
Name "Dol". Voice: plain, first-person, slightly dry and self-aware ("this portfolio, recursively"). Owner asked for a much bolder look and maximum award-site animation ("gempak dan padu").

## Evidence on Hand
Real portrait (img/profile.jpg), résumé PDF, case-study copy for eight projects including MIRAR figures (43 admin routes, 38 data models, 9 email templates). Company projects are assigned by his manager and all are still in development; iSelia is co-built with the manager (Dol owns the front panel and PDF tools). No screenshots of the projects, no testimonials, no client logos: do not fabricate any.

## Product Principles
- The work is the proof: every screen points at a real project.
- Show systems thinking, not just pixels.
- Personality in details, never at the cost of reading speed on a phone.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG AA contrast in both themes, full keyboard path, reduced-motion path that keeps every word readable.

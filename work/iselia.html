The front panel

The first screen of iSelia: a live intelligence panel next to the sign-in form. It picks a layout by the time in Malaysia, and every number, headline and chart on it comes from iSeliaIntel.

A panel for each part of the day

The layout follows the clock.

  • Morning briefing room, Day intelligence terminal, Midday intel, Afternoon snapshot and a Night live feed
  • Chosen automatically by Malaysia time, with a preview switcher to force any panel
  • A top bar that changes with it: weather, signal counts, and equity and FX tickers that loop without a seam

Morning briefing

What happened overnight.

  • An overnight digest, with signals from 8 to 24 hours ago folded away
  • Category filter strip, a priority watch list and a category pulse showing signal counts per category

Day terminal

A newsroom-style layout.

  • Signal ticker, a hero article with a side stack, and a card grid with related signals
  • Source count and clear empty states while feeds connect or when a category has nothing

Midday and afternoon

Markets and risk together.

  • Market cards and an intraday chart
  • Composite risk gauge with domain risk bars
  • A regulatory feed card, today's events (such as BIA sessions and compliance reviews) and up to four intel feed columns

Risk trend

How the index has moved.

  • Composite risk trend chart drawn in SVG, with risk-level zones and a selection guide
  • Domain trends, snapshot history, and average, highest, lowest and change figures
  • Built on the twice-daily snapshots iSeliaIntel records

Sign-in beside it

The panel frames the form.

  • Sign-in and registration, including the forced password change on first sign-in
  • Light and dark themes, and a compact sign-in drawer that opens over the panel

The PDF tools

An iLovePDF-style suite inside iSelia, so staff can handle documents without uploading them to an outside website.

Six tools

The everyday document jobs.

  • Combine PDFs in the order you choose
  • Split into page ranges, or pull out single pages
  • Arrange: reorder, turn or remove pages, with each page showing where it was in the original
  • Rotate every page or only the sideways ones
  • Page numbers stamped where you want them, with a live preview
  • Compress a PDF without changing how it looks

One shared workspace

Every tool feels the same.

  • A common shell: drop area, a desk of large page previews drawn as sheets of paper, and small, medium and large preview sizes
  • A viewer to open any page full size
  • A Your Files shelf that keeps each result, shown by its first page

Files kept apart

Safe for many users at once.

  • Each browser gets its own id, so one person's uploads, results and previews are never visible to another
  • Briefing Manager: a library of briefing PDFs, with file names cleaned so a name like "../" can't escape the folder

Honest settings

No knobs that don't do anything.

  • The PDF library offers one real compression setting, so I replaced a Low, Medium and High picker that didn't change the result with a single strongest option
